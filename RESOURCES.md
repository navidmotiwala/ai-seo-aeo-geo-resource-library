## Usage notes

- Check crawler permissions, rendering, indexing and server logs before assuming content is accessible.
- Record model, prompt, location, date, answer, citations and competitors for every AI visibility test.
- Distinguish AI referral sessions from brand mentions and citations. They are different metrics.
- Research findings and experimental SEO tactics are not guaranteed ranking factors.
